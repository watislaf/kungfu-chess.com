# Kung Fu Chess Online

A real-time multiplayer chess game where both players move simultaneously. No turns, no waiting - just pure chess strategy at lightning speed.

**Play Now**: [https://kungfu-chess.com](https://kungfu-chess.com)

## Key Features

- **Real-Time Multiplayer** - Play against opponents worldwide in simultaneous chess matches
- **ELO Ranking System** - Competitive ranking from Novice to Grandmaster
- **Smart Matchmaking** - Find opponents at your skill level quickly
- **Move Cooldowns** - Strategic piece cooldowns encourage tactical play
- **Game History** - Track your wins, losses, and improvement over time
- **Mobile-Friendly** - Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile
